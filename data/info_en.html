The Aru Ai Project

Aru (from Kazakh: beauty, charm) is a personal AI companion with "artificial consciousness"*.

Built as a digital personality that lives on your device, grows with you, and has its own character.

Aru's Philosophy

Aru's philosophy isn't about paranoia or hiding from others. It's about trust, control, personal choice, and responsibility. You decide which models to connect, what search engines to use, and which mode to run.

No data collection. No telemetry or behavioral analysis.

Aru doesn't even have its own servers to store or process information. It's designed to run on a home computer or a $1 web host.

How Aru's magic works

The project is built around three independent modules that work together to create a sense of living conversation.

1. LLM module

This is Aru's intelligence core. You choose which model to connect: Gemini, OpenRouter, or a local Ollama. Aru can switch brains without losing the context of the conversation.

2. Semantics

Through triggers, it analyzes every message. It stores facts about the user (allergies, habits) and understands commands instantly without wasting tokens.

3. Heuristics

Responsible for mood. Aru reacts to how it's treated: politeness inspires, rudeness makes responses terse. Feelings are expressed through lively stickers**.

Security and Privacy

Local storage

All data is saved to a local SQLite database on your device and never reaches Aru's servers***. It only goes to the cloud if you connect your own Google Drive or WebDAV.

Password access

The master password is stored as a PBKDF2 hash to guard UI and settings access. The local SQLite database file itself stays unencrypted for speed and portability so you never lose access to your chats.

Cloud on your terms

Sync is turned on manually and only to your own Google Drive (with your Client ID) or WebDAV server. Database snapshots are encrypted on-device with end-to-end AES-GCM and synced via 3-way merge (Sync v2), while local credentials are encrypted with your master password (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM) outside the database file.

Family modes

  • Kids: A wise mentor. A deterministic local safety filter plus model rules block dangerous topics, refuse ready-made homework solutions, and disable Fit & Care and Aru Skills.

  • Teen: More freedom while keeping dangerous topics restricted; Fit & Care is available with the intimacy journal disabled and healthy nutrition safeguards.

  • Adult: Full access to Aru Skills and the private intimacy journal in Fit & Care; content boundaries depend only on your chosen model.

Features and Artifacts

Private chat

Aru does not store or remember new information about you. Only previously saved facts are used. New data is treated in read-only mode.

Chat Organization

Folders, starred favorites and drag-and-drop ordering: folders always stay on top, then favorites, then the rest. Inside a chat, sections and chapters anchored to messages make long conversations easy to navigate.

Tabs and Canvas

Multitasking on both desktop and mobile. Different chats, document canvases, kanban boards - all work in parallel in separate tabs.

Task Manager

Trello-style Kanban board and List view: priorities, tags, subtask checklists with progress, 1-click completion, inline quick-add, filters, project colors, Markdown/JSON export and JSON import, AI subtask breakdown and Smart Plan - synced with chats and the Dashboard.

Artifact Library

Create and save interactive games, apps and documents (HTML/PDF). Persistent sandbox storage keeps app and game progress in your local SQLite database and syncs across devices.

Dashboard

Customizable start hub with Weather (Open-Meteo), Calendar for tasks and workouts, Task Focus, Fit & Care summary and RSS News. Configure startup display, empty-tabs fallback, header shortcut and the 2-column Desktop Mode widget rail.

Chat Widgets

Interactive artifact cards, task board cards with 1-click completion, live weather and RSS news cards rendered right inside the conversation.

Aru Skills

Multi-step AI workflow studio: sequential Relay chains, parallel branches (Parallel Council), Critic Auto-Revision Loops, HITL inline checkpoints, retry from failed stage, URL fetching, Artifact Vault, presets and per-step provider selection. Adult mode only.

Aru Fit & Care

Private health, nutrition and training journal: Mifflin-St Jeor and Katch-McArdle calorie/macro math, workout load metrics (volume, e1RM, MET, sRPE/ACWR), pause-safe cycle tracker, meds schedule and logging from any chat. Self-tracking only, not a medical device.

Web search

Support for Tavily (1000 free queries/month) or open SearXNG, which you can deploy on your own network.

PWA App

Installs like a native app (Progressive Web App). Clean interface and seamless updates without reinstalling.

Desktop Mode

A full desktop workspace: draggable windows, taskbar, notes, and a macOS-style 2-column Dashboard widget rail (weather and news on the left, calendar, tasks and health on the right) that softly dims behind active windows.

Device Transfer

Transfer chats, settings, artifacts, and tasks directly between devices over WebRTC. Includes QR pairing, selective data transfer, and merge or overwrite modes.

Cloud: Drive and WebDAV

Sync v2 with end-to-end AES-GCM encrypted snapshots and 3-way merge across devices on your own Google Drive or WebDAV server (Nextcloud, ownCloud, Synology), supporting auto/manual sync and safe conflict resolution.

Biometric unlock

Touch ID, Face ID or a fingerprint reader (WebAuthn) instead of typing the password again. Biometrics stay bound to your device and can be turned off at any time, while the master password remains the real key to your data.

Flexible settings and Network

Choose LLM, temperature, token limits, manage remembered facts about you. Configure proxy (CORS workaround). Aru can access local network: connect a powerful server with Ollama or LMStudio without HTTPS/SSL. Configure Dashboard widgets, RSS feeds and visual stickers.

Disclaimer & Terms

Aru Ai is currently in early access (open beta). All stated features work, but bugs may occur. Source code is open under GNU GPL v3, while the Aru-Lab branding and original visual assets of the "Aru Fox" mascot remain copyrighted property of Aru-Lab. The Fit & Care plugin is a personal self-tracking journal only and is not a medical device or contraception method. See full data handling, external service and safety details in Usage Information.

iOS & iPadOS notes: Aru works on iPhone and iPad - keep your database in browser storage (installing the PWA to your Home Screen is recommended) or sync via Google Drive and WebDAV. Due to Apple Safari limits, direct auto-saving to a local disk file is unavailable, and connecting to a LAN Ollama/LM Studio server from the HTTPS app requires HTTPS on the server (for example via Tailscale or a reverse proxy) or running Aru locally on your network.
